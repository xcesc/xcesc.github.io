Francesco Di Giuseppe

PhD Candidate in Statistics

TU Delft

I am a PhD candidate in Mathematical Statistics at TU Delft, working under the supervision of Prof. Aad van der Vaart and Professor Geurt Jongbloed. My research interests are Causal Inference and Bayesian Statistics, with particular attention to: Latent Variables, Missing Data and Structured/Constrained models.

I am also currently the chairperson of the Young Statisticians of the Netherlands (VVSOR) --- you can find out more about the events we organize there.

Outside research, I really enjoy traveling, making pizza, brewing coffee, and running. I am very grateful that my job allows me to do all of the above.
